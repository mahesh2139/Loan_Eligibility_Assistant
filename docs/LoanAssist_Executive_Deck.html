Loan Eligibility Assistant — Problem & Business Case

Pre-qualification conversations consume agent time and produce inconsistent answers across changing policy versions.

SLIDE 01
1 · Operational Pain

Multi-Document, Moving Rules

Eligibility depends on 9–11 interacting criteria (Age, Income, Vintage, CIBIL, FOIR, LTV, Asset Type) spread across product policies (personal_loan_v1/v2, home_loan_v1, auto_loan_v1) that update on independent cycles.

2 · Cost of the Status Quo

Complaints vs. Lost Lending Business

  • False Positive (Told Eligible when Not): Triggers customer complaints, compliance risk, and wasted underwriting effort.
  • False Negative (Told Ineligible when Eligible): Silent lost business. Neither failure is visible until after the fact.
3 · Why Generic LLMs Fail

Hallucinated Cut-Offs & Math Errors

Generic LLMs invent credit thresholds, mix stale policy versions, and miscalculate reducing-balance EMI, FOIR, and LTV ratios. A confidently wrong income or LTV cut-off is a regulatory failure, not a UX bug.

OUR ARCHITECTURAL THESIS — STRICT NEURO-SYMBOLIC SEPARATION OF RESPONSIBILITIES
① Semantic NLU + Memory

Natural Intent Routing

Infers product without UI tags ("house/building loan" → Home; "car/scooter/bus" → Auto) & merges profile across turns without re-asking.

② Versioned Policy RAG

Active-Version Filtering

Clause-level ChromaDB collections queried with where={"version": active} so every answer cites the exact active Rule ID.

③ Deterministic Engine

LLM Never Decides

Pure-Python calculators (EMI, FOIR, LTV, Max Loan) + 25 rule evaluators produce 100% deterministic pass/fail decisions.

④ Governed Delivery

SSE Stream + CI Gate

Sentence-buffered output guardrails, full rule-version audit logging, and Promptfoo CI/CD gates that block regressions.

✅ IN SCOPE (IMPLEMENTED END-TO-END)

Streaming pre-qualification across Personal Loan (v1/v2), Home Loan (v1), and Auto Loan (v1) with grounded Rule-ID citations, What-If scenario simulator, on-demand document checklists, and version-stamped audit logs.

🚫 EXPLICIT COMPLIANCE BOUNDARY

Never claims final loan approval or sanction. Outputs are strictly preliminary pre-qualification outcomes: POTENTIALLY_ELIGIBLE, NOT_ELIGIBLE, MANUAL_REVIEW, or INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION.

1 · TWO-STAGE CI/CD EVAL GATE

.github/workflows/eval-gate.yml

  • Stage 1 — unit-tests (Every Push): Runs pytest api/tests/ across all branches in ~10s without Docker overhead.
  • Stage 2 — eval-gate (Pull Requests): Ingests ChromaDB policies, boots the Docker stack, waits for /health, and executes scripts/eval-gate.sh (Promptfoo).
  • Blocked Merge Evidence: Uploads promptfoo-output.json/.html artifacts and blocks PR merge on any accuracy drop.
2 · THREE-LAYER SAFETY DEFENCE

Boundary & Mid-Stream Protection

  • Layer 1 (Input Guard): Blocks prompt-injection phrases ("ignore instructions"), masks PII, and enforces domain topic gate.
  • Layer 2 (Grounded LLM): Versioned prompts (registry.yaml v1/v2) at temperature=0; strictly subordinate to engine output.
  • Layer 3 (Sentence Stream Guard): Buffers streamed tokens by sentence and strips approval guarantees or leaked PII before SSE flush.

Honest Limitations, Production Roadmap & Rubric Alignment

Clear engineering trade-offs in the current release and the path to enterprise banking production.

SLIDE 05
⚠️ HONEST LIMITATIONS (WHAT IT DOES NOT DO YET)
  • In-Memory Session & Idempotency Stores: _conv_store, _profile_store, and _idem_cache reside in FastAPI process memory (cleared on container restart; single-instance bound).
  • Dual-Place Policy Thresholds: Switching ACTIVE_PERSONAL_LOAN_VERSION=v2 dynamically updates RAG retrieval chunks, whereas rules_engine.py reads thresholds from Python config dicts.
  • Unverified Applicant Inputs: Uses self-declared income, CIBIL score, and property/vehicle value — no live bureau pull or document OCR.
  • Rule-Based Boundary Guardrails: Uses deterministic regex and keyword allowlists rather than ML safety classifiers.
🚀 ENTERPRISE PRODUCTION ROADMAP (NEXT STEPS)
  • Single Source-of-Truth Policy Schema: Drive both ChromaDB RAG ingestion and rules_engine.py config thresholds from a single signed YAML/JSON policy file per version.
  • Externalized State & WORM Audit Storage: Move session/idempotency state to Redis, vector search to managed Qdrant / pgvector, and audit logs to append-only PostgreSQL + S3 Object Lock.
  • Core Banking & Bureau Integration: Integrate soft-pull CIBIL API, Account Aggregator (AA) income verification, and LOS lead-handoff webhooks.
  • GPU Serving & Neural Guardrails: Serve 7B/8B models on vLLM with Microsoft Presidio NER and Llama-Guard 3 classifiers.

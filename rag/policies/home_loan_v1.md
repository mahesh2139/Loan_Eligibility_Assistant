# Home Loan Eligibility Policy — v1

> **Disclaimer**: This is a synthetic policy document created for demonstration
> purposes only. Thresholds do not represent any RBI directive or NHB regulation.

## 1. Age Eligibility

**Rule ID:** HL-AGE-001

Applicants must be at least 21 years old at the time of loan application.

The loan must close (final instalment paid) before the applicant reaches
70 years of age. Accordingly, the maximum permissible tenure is determined
as: (70 − applicant age) × 12 months.

For example, a 45-year-old applicant may take a loan for a maximum of
300 months (25 years), not the product maximum of 360 months.

## 2. Minimum Income

**Rule ID:** HL-INC-001

Applicants must have a minimum monthly net income of ₹40,000 (forty thousand
rupees) after all statutory deductions.

For joint applicants, the combined monthly net income is considered.

Income below the stated threshold does not meet the minimum requirement.
Income must be verified through salary slips, bank statements, or ITR
before eligibility can be determined.

## 3. Employment Stability

**Rule ID:** HL-EMP-001

Salaried applicants must demonstrate at least 24 months (2 years) of
continuous employment, including at least 6 months with the current employer.

Self-employed individuals and business owners must demonstrate at least
36 months (3 years) of continuous business operation, supported by audited
financials, IT returns, and business bank statements.

Self-employed applicants with 24–35 months of business operation require
additional documentation and are subject to manual underwriting review.

Self-employed applicants with fewer than 24 months of business operation
do not meet the employment stability requirement.

## 4. Credit Score

**Rule ID:** HL-CRD-001

Applicants must have a minimum credit score of 700 (on a scale of 300–900)
from an RBI-approved credit information bureau.

For joint applications, both co-applicants must individually meet this
minimum score unless a guarantor arrangement is in place.

A credit score below 700 does not meet the eligibility requirement.

## 5. Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)

**Rule ID:** HL-FOIR-001

The Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) — calculated as all monthly
loan EMIs (including the proposed home loan EMI) divided by monthly net
income — must not exceed 55%.

FOIR = (Existing Monthly EMIs + Proposed Home Loan EMI) ÷ Monthly Net Income ≤ 0.55

A FOIR exceeding 55% indicates that the applicant's existing debt burden
is too high relative to their income for an additional home loan.

## 6. Loan-to-Value Ratio (LTV)

**Rule ID:** HL-LTV-001

The Loan-to-Value ratio determines the maximum loan as a percentage of
the property's assessed market value:

- For loan amounts up to ₹30,00,000 (thirty lakh rupees): maximum LTV of 85%
- For loan amounts above ₹30,00,000 (thirty lakh rupees): maximum LTV of 80%

LTV = Loan Amount ÷ Property Market Value

The down payment must cover the remaining portion (at least 15%–20% of
property value).

Example: For a property valued at ₹50,00,000 with a loan request of ₹42,00,000,
LTV = 42L / 50L = 84%. Since the loan exceeds ₹30L, maximum LTV is 80%,
so the maximum eligible loan is ₹40,00,000. This loan does not meet LTV rules.

## 7. Property Eligibility

**Rule ID:** HL-PROP-001

Financing is available only for the following property types:

- Apartment (flat in a residential complex)
- Villa (standalone residential property with defined plot)
- Residential house (independent house on a defined plot)

The following property types are NOT eligible:

- Commercial property
- Agricultural land
- Plots (land without construction)
- Properties with unclear title or encumbrances
- Properties in non-approved areas

The property must have a clear title and must be free from encumbrances.
Property eligibility is subject to lender's technical and legal evaluation.

## 8. Loan Amount

**Rule ID:** HL-AMT-001

The minimum home loan amount is ₹5,00,000 (five lakh rupees).
The maximum home loan amount is ₹5,00,00,000 (five crore rupees).

The loan amount is further capped by:
- The applicable LTV ratio applied to the property's assessed value
- The maximum affordable amount calculated from the applicant's income
  and FOIR constraint

## 9. Loan Tenure

**Rule ID:** HL-TEN-001

Home loan tenure must be between 12 months (1 year) and 360 months (30 years),
subject to the age-based maximum tenure described in Section 1.

Longer tenures reduce the monthly EMI burden but increase total interest paid.

## 10. Documentation Requirements

**Rule ID:** HL-DOC-001

The following documents are required for a home loan application:

- Identity proof: Aadhaar card and PAN card (mandatory)
- Address proof: utility bill, bank statement, or registered rental agreement
- Income proof:
  - Salaried: last 3 months pay slips, Form 16 for last 2 years
  - Self-employed: last 2 years audited financial statements and ITR
- Bank statements: last 12 months (salaried) / last 24 months (self-employed)
- Property documents:
  - Sale agreement or allotment letter
  - Title deed / chain of title documents
  - Approved building plan
  - NOC from housing society or builder
  - Property tax receipts

## 11. Exceptions and Special Cases

**Rule ID:** HL-EXC-001

The following cases require manual underwriting review:

- Self-employed applicants with 24–35 months of business operation
- Co-applicant arrangements where one co-applicant has a credit score
  between 680 and 699
- Properties with partial construction or in pre-launch projects
- NRI applicants (additional documentation and FEMA compliance required)
- Properties valued above ₹3 crore (enhanced due diligence required)

Pre-qualification does not constitute final approval. Final approval is
subject to property valuation, legal verification, and credit underwriting.

# Auto Loan Eligibility Policy -- v1

> **Disclaimer**: This is a synthetic policy document for demonstration only.
> It does not represent any RBI directive or regulation.

## 1. Age Eligibility

**Rule ID:** AL-AGE-001

Applicants must be at least 21 years old at the time of application.
The maximum age at the time of loan application is 65 years. The loan
must be fully repaid before the applicant reaches 70 years of age.

For example, a 60-year-old applicant may take a loan for a maximum of
120 months (10 years) subject to other product limits.

## 2. Minimum Income

**Rule ID:** AL-INC-001

Applicants must have a minimum monthly net income of Rs.20,000 (twenty
thousand rupees) after all statutory deductions.

For self-employed and business applicants, average monthly net profit
over the last 12 months must meet the minimum threshold.

## 3. Employment and Business Stability

**Rule ID:** AL-EMP-001

Salaried applicants must demonstrate a minimum of 12 months of continuous
employment, including at least 6 months with the current employer.

Self-employed individuals and business owners must demonstrate at least
24 months of continuous business operation, supported by IT returns and
business bank statements.

Freshers and probationary employees are not eligible.

## 4. Credit Score

**Rule ID:** AL-CRD-001

Applicants must have a minimum credit score of 680 (on a scale of 300-900)
from an RBI-approved credit information bureau.

A credit score below 680 does not meet the eligibility requirement.
Applicants with scores between 680 and 699 may be subject to enhanced
documentation or a higher margin requirement.

## 5. Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)

**Rule ID:** AL-FOIR-001

The Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) including the proposed auto
loan EMI must not exceed 50% of monthly net income.

FOIR = (Existing Monthly EMIs + Proposed Auto Loan EMI) / Monthly Net Income <= 0.50

Applicants with FOIR exceeding 50% do not meet the debt serviceability
requirement and are not eligible for an auto loan.

## 6. Loan-to-Value Ratio (LTV)

**Rule ID:** AL-LTV-001

The Loan-to-Value ratio is calculated on the on-road price of the vehicle
(ex-showroom price plus registration, insurance, and taxes).

For new vehicles: maximum LTV of 85% of on-road price.
For used/pre-owned vehicles: maximum LTV of 70% of on-road price.

The applicant must arrange the remaining amount as a down payment or
own contribution from personal funds.

Used vehicles must not be more than 10 years old at the time of loan
application, and must not exceed 15 years of age at loan maturity.

## 7. Vehicle Eligibility

**Rule ID:** AL-VEH-001

The following vehicle types are eligible for financing:

- Two-wheelers: motorcycles and scooters (new and used)
- Four-wheelers: passenger cars, SUVs, MUVs (new and used)
- Commercial vehicles: trucks, buses, vans used for business (new only)
- Electric vehicles (EVs): all categories (new only; used EVs require manual review)

The following are NOT eligible:

- Racing vehicles and modified vehicles
- Vehicles older than 10 years (at the time of application)
- Agricultural vehicles and tractors (covered under separate schemes)
- Imported grey-market vehicles without clear customs documentation

## 8. Loan Amount

**Rule ID:** AL-AMT-001

The minimum auto loan amount is Rs.50,000 (fifty thousand rupees).
The maximum auto loan amount is Rs.50,00,000 (fifty lakh rupees).

The loan amount is further limited by:
- The applicable LTV ratio applied to the on-road vehicle price
- The maximum affordable amount based on the applicant's income and FOIR

## 9. Loan Tenure

**Rule ID:** AL-TEN-001

Auto loan tenure must be between 12 months (1 year) and 84 months (7 years) for new vehicles.

For used vehicles, the maximum tenure is 60 months (5 years).

For commercial vehicles, the maximum tenure is 48 months (4 years).

The total tenure must not cause the vehicle age to exceed 15 years at
the time of the final instalment.

## 10. Documentation Requirements

**Rule ID:** AL-DOC-001

The following documents are required for an auto loan application:

For all applicants:
- Identity proof: Aadhaar card and PAN card (mandatory)
- Address proof: utility bill, bank statement, or registered rental agreement
- Last 6 months bank statements

For salaried applicants:
- Last 3 months pay slips
- Form 16 for last 2 years
- Employment letter or appointment letter

For self-employed/business applicants:
- Last 2 years IT returns with computation
- Last 6 months business bank statements
- Business registration certificate or GST registration

For vehicle:
- Proforma invoice or quotation from dealer (new vehicles)
- RC book, insurance certificate, and valuation report (used vehicles)

## 11. Exceptions and Manual Review Cases

**Rule ID:** AL-EXC-001

The following cases are subject to manual underwriting review:

- Used electric vehicles (EVs) financed for the first time
- Commercial vehicle loans above Rs.20 lakh for new borrowers
- Applicants with credit scores between 660 and 679 with strong income
- Applicants with FOIR between 50% and 55% with excellent credit (750+)
- Fleet financing for more than one vehicle

Manual review does not guarantee approval. Pre-qualification is not
a commitment to lend.

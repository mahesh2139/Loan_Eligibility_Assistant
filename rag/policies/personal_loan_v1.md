# Personal Loan Eligibility Policy — v1

> **Disclaimer**: This is a synthetic policy document created for demonstration
> purposes only. Thresholds do not represent any RBI directive or regulation.

## 1. Age Eligibility

**Rule ID:** PL-AGE-001

Applicants must be at least 21 years old and not older than 60 years at the
time of loan application.

Applicants below 21 or above 60 do not meet the age eligibility requirement.
Age must be confirmed before an assessment can be completed.

## 2. Minimum Income

**Rule ID:** PL-INC-001

Applicants must have a minimum monthly net income of ₹25,000 (twenty-five
thousand rupees) after all statutory deductions.

Gross annual income converted to monthly net income by the applicant must
be verifiable through pay slips, bank statements, or Form 16.

Income below the stated threshold does not meet the minimum requirement.

## 3. Employment Duration

**Rule ID:** PL-EMP-001

Salaried applicants must demonstrate a minimum of 12 months of continuous
employment with the current employer.

Self-employed individuals and business owners must demonstrate at least
24 months of continuous business operation, supported by business income
proof, IT returns, and bank statements.

Employment status and duration must be established before eligibility can
be determined.

## 4. Credit Score

**Rule ID:** PL-CRD-001

Applicants must have a minimum credit score of 700 (on a scale of 300–900)
from an RBI-approved credit information bureau.

A credit score below 700 does not meet the credit eligibility requirement.
Credit status must be verified before an assessment can be completed.

## 5. Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)

**Rule ID:** PL-FOIR-001

The Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) — calculated as the sum of all
monthly loan EMIs (including the proposed new EMI) divided by monthly net
income — must not exceed 50%.

FOIR = (Existing Monthly EMIs + Proposed New EMI) ÷ Monthly Net Income ≤ 0.50

An applicant whose FOIR exceeds 50% does not meet the debt obligation
requirement. The calculation requires income, requested amount, and tenure.

## 6. Loan Amount

**Rule ID:** PL-AMT-001

The minimum personal loan amount is ₹50,000 (fifty thousand rupees).
The maximum personal loan amount is ₹25,00,000 (twenty-five lakh rupees).

Additionally, the loan amount must not exceed 30 times the applicant's
monthly net income.

Loan amounts outside this range or exceeding the income multiplier cap
do not meet the loan amount requirement.

## 7. Loan Tenure

**Rule ID:** PL-TEN-001

Personal loan tenure must be between 12 months and 60 months (1 to 5 years).

Tenures outside this range are not eligible under this policy.

## 8. Documentation Requirements

**Rule ID:** PL-DOC-001

The following documents are required for a personal loan application:

- Identity proof (Aadhaar card, PAN card, passport, or driving licence)
- Address proof (utility bill, rental agreement, or bank statement)
- Income proof: last 3 months pay slips (salaried) or last 2 years ITR (self-employed)
- Bank statements: last 6 months
- Form 16 (salaried applicants)
- Business registration certificate and GST registration (self-employed)

## 9. Exceptions and Special Cases

**Rule ID:** PL-EXC-001

The following cases require manual underwriting review:

- Self-employed applicants with 12–23 months of business operation
- Applicants with credit scores between 680 and 699 who have demonstrated
  strong income growth over 12 months
- Applicants with FOIR between 50% and 55% with excellent credit (750+)

Manual review does not guarantee approval. A human underwriter will assess
the complete application.
